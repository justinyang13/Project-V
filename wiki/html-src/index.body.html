Scene idea → 1-hour ambient loop video

Give Claude a one-line scene idea. It generates the image (you pick from 4), analyses the scene, animates only what should move (snow, fire, steam…), builds a seamless 10-second loop, composes original piano music with quiet ambience, and delivers a one-hour 1080p video plus the audio and the silent loop.

Rules that make it work

  1. Only what should move comes from AI. Everything else is the original image, pixel-locked by a mask.
  2. Build the loop, don't generate it. Two clips from the same image, chained A → B → A.
  3. Measure the camera first. Models zoom and drift; check on 3 seeds before rendering anything final.
  4. Describe a photograph, not a scene. "One single locked photograph… the only motion is…". Words like "the camera never zooms" made drift worse.
  5. One heavy model at a time on the 64 GB Mac (video, music, LLMs never together).
  6. Numbers gate every stage; Claude can't hear or watch, so you make the approvals and reviews.
  7. Approve first, render later: image, what animates and the audio style are approved before any long render.
  8. Soft to the ear, verified: every music piece is machine-checked and the hour comes with a listening pack.
  9. Design the image for animation: a view out through a doorway or window, with the moving things on soft backgrounds, is easy to mask.
