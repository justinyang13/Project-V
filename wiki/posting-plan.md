# Posting plan — Zen Hour Music (first 12 weeks)

Written 2026-09-23. Goal: build a channel that looks and feels hand-made (varied, consistent, cared for) while working toward the Partner Program (1,000 subscribers + 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months). Nothing here is a growth guarantee; treat the numbers as targets to check against YouTube Analytics and adjust.

## 1. The rules this plan follows
1. **Vary every upload.** Different scene, season, weather or music style each time. Never two near-identical videos in a row. This is the main defence against YouTube's "inauthentic content" (mass-produced / repetitive) policy, which is judged across the whole channel.
2. **Steady, not flooding.** Two long videos a week, plus a few Shorts. Regular looks like a real channel; 10 uploads in a day looks like a content farm.
3. **Series, not random uploads.** Every video belongs to a named series and a playlist, so a viewer who likes one finds the next.
4. **Timing matches the season.** Autumn scenes now, snow from November, spring blossoms from March.
5. **Human touch on every video:** a written description, a pinned comment, replies to comments.
6. **Only promise scenes the pipeline can make.** See §6; new effects (rain, water, leaves) need testing first.

## 6. Pipeline work needed before 🟡 videos
In order of payoff (see SPEC §12):
1. **Rain ambience** synthesizer + rain motion test (unlocks the whole Rainy Zen series).
2. **Falling leaves** motion test (Autumn Temple).
3. **Water / pond ripple** and **mist** motion tests; **night insects** and **birds** ambience (Night Garden).
4. **Validate the Draw Things CLI** (standing rule; SPEC stage 0).
5. **Music variety:** keep piano as the anchor, but add koto and flute variants so the channel doesn't sound identical every video.
Each is one short test with Claude before it's used in a published video.

## 7. Every upload: checklist
- [ ] New scene; different from the previous two uploads
- [ ] Title follows the pattern `{Scene} ❄/🍁/🌧 {Music} & {Ambience} | 1 Hour {Use}` — but vary the wording; don't copy-paste
- [ ] Thumbnail: clean frame of the scene, 1280×720, big short text ("1 HOUR", 2–3 words), readable on a phone
- [ ] Description: first two lines describe the scene; what you'll hear; when to play; AI disclosure; 3 hashtags first
- [ ] Studio: **Altered or synthetic content = Yes**, Not made for kids, Category Music, added to its series playlist
- [ ] Pinned comment asking where people listen from / what scene next
- [ ] End screen → next video in the same series + subscribe
- [ ] Short (Wed/Sun): 9:16 crop of the loop with 20–30 s of the music, title with a hook, linked to the full video ("Related video")

## 8. Shorts plan
- 2 a week, one per long video, made from the silent 10-second loop (crop 9:16, loop 2–3×, add a music excerpt).
- Titles like "POV: snowy tea room at 2 am ❄" or "Close your eyes for 20 seconds 🍵".
- Always link the full hour as the related video. Shorts views don't count toward the 4,000 watch hours, but they bring subscribers.

## 9. Community (from week 3)
- Every other Sunday: a poll ("Next scene: rain 🌧 / autumn 🍁 / night 🌙"). Then make the winner and say so in its description — viewers who asked for it come back.
- Answer every comment in the first 48 hours after publishing.

## 10. Things not to do
- Re-upload the same video with a new title, or loop one video into 10 hours and call it new.
- Copy other channels' titles or thumbnails, or use real place names for imagined scenes.
- Buy views/subscribers or join "sub4sub" groups (can get the channel banned and ruins the Analytics).
- Stuff long ad breaks into sleep videos once monetized.
