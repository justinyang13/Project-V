# ASTRA: "The Last Lantern" (story, v1, 2026-10-19)

Written by Claude from three qwen3.8:27b drafts (`drafts/qwen_loglines.md`). Kept: the theme of letting go from draft 1. Dropped: abstract action (memory waves, emotion plants, sound ribbons), which reads weakly and is hard for LTX.

## Logline
In a world whose sun has died, an android companion carries her dead maker's last flame to the edge of the world and learns that the light she was built to protect was never in the lantern.

## Theme
What makes someone alive is not what they are made of but what they choose to give. Astra was built to keep and carry; she becomes human (and becomes her maker's successor, the lamplighter) at the moment she gives away the light that keeps her running. Tagline from the sheet, earned by the ending: *a companion for what's next*: she lights a dawn she may not live to see.

## Want vs need
- **Want:** finish her task: deliver the flame, keep it alive, survive the hunters.
- **Need:** understand that the flame is a symbol; the real fire is her own light, and giving it is the point.

## World
A high, ancient mountain kingdom under a dead, pale sun. Ash falls like snow. The Grey (living ash) rises as faceless wraiths with ember eyes that hunt any light. Her maker, the last lamplighter (an old man, seen only from behind, in silhouette, or as hands), built Astra in his tower observatory. At the world's edge stands the Sun Cradle, a colossal stone ring with a brass brazier where the sun was first lit.

## The visual through-line (light = life)
- Warm amber flame in a brass lantern (the maker's fire) vs Astra's cool blue LED lines (her own life).
- The flame shrinks across the film; at the bridge she feeds it a thread of her blue light (first cost; her cheek chevron dims). At the Cradle the flame is dead; she gives all of her blue light; her lines go dark from the feet up; the ring ignites; a new gold sun rises; warm sunlight falls on her dark face. Final beat: the chevron flickers back once and her eyes open. The dawn gives back.

## Structure (≈3:03, 40 shots, see SHOTLIST.json)
1. **The Tower (0:00-0:45).** She wakes in candlelight. Her maker gives her the lantern, then dies quietly (his candle goes out). She steps out into falling ash under a dead sun.
2. **The Grey Wastes (0:45-1:27).** A valley of fallen colossal statues. She shields the flame from the wind. Ash wraiths rise. Blue energy blade from her gauntlet; ash explodes; more rise; she runs for the bridge.
3. **The Broken Bridge (1:27-2:08).** An ancient bridge over a chasm in an ash storm. Stones fall; she lands hard, armor cracked, one LED line dying. The flame is almost out: she cups it and gives it a thread of her light. The swarm closes; she drives her blade into the bridge; the shockwave throws them into the abyss with the falling span.
4. **The World's Edge (2:08-3:03).** Blue hour above an endless cloud sea; the Sun Cradle. She kneels, opens the lantern: dead wick. Stillness. She presses her palm to her chest; her light flows into the brazier; she goes dark; the ring ignites; a new sun rises; gold light on her still face; the chevron returns; her eyes open. Title: ASTRA.

## Performance notes
Composed, economical, protective. Emotion is shown in stillness, eyes, the chevron's pulse and the LED lines, never in big facial expressions (big expressions drift the face). No dialogue, no narration.
