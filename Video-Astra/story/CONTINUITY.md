# Astra continuity ledger (RULE 0c)

Major items and rules (full per-shot ledger in `ledger.json`):

- **lantern**: old brass hurricane lantern, glass panes, bail handle, ~30 cm tall, small warm flame INSIDE the glass only. Rules: never a torch; flame stays inside the glass; one lantern only.
- **energy_blade**: straight rigid blade of bright blue energy from her open RIGHT palm, ~1 m long, glowing cyan. Rules: always perfectly straight, no sword hilt, appears only B06-C09/C08-C09 shots.
- **ash_wraiths**: tall faceless humanoids of dark grey ash, ember-orange eyes, edges dissolving. Rules: die by dissolving into fine drifting ash that vanishes; no explosions, blobs or fireballs.
- **maker**: old man, dark-brown wool robe, grey hair. Rules: seen from behind only, never his face; only in tower A shots.
- **astra_state**: S0 clean -> S1 ash dust (B) -> S2 + cracked right shoulder plate (C) -> S3 dim LEDs/marks (D01-D06) -> S4 LEDs/marks dark (D07-D09), marks relight in D10. Rules: see shot state field.
- **brazier_ring**: wide brass brazier on a stone dais at the foot of a 30 m stone sun-ring on the cliff top. Rules: same ring and brazier in every D shot; ring ignites in D08.
- **bridge**: narrow ancient stone bridge, broken balustrades over a chasm. Rules: collapses in C03 (stones fall); wraiths and Astra on it in C shots.
