# World bible: The Last Lantern (4 locations, locked)

Each location block goes into every keyframe prompt of that sequence, word for word. Lighting plans follow the cinematic quality bar (SKILL.md).

## L1. The Lamplighter's Tower (seq A)
**Look:** circular stone observatory room at the top of a mountain tower; walls of rough grey stone; a great arched window of leaded glass; tall brass clockwork and gear assemblies, hanging brass lanterns (unlit), shelves of old books and tools, a heavy oak workbench with candles; a low stone table where Astra lay. Outside the window: grey sky, falling ash, a pale dead sun disc.
**Light:** low-key interior. Key = warm candlelight (2700 K) from the workbench, low and to the side, 6:1 key:fill. Rim = cool grey daylight through the arched window behind. Astra's blue LEDs add cyan accents on her face and armor. Haze: thin dust in the window light shafts. Palette: amber, deep brown shadows, slate blue.
**Props (size):** brass lantern (hand-held, 30 cm tall, glass panes, small warm flame), candles 15-25 cm, workbench 2 m long.
**Maker:** an old man in a worn dark-brown wool robe, grey hair; seen only from behind, in silhouette against the window, or as weathered hands. Never his face.

## L2. The Grey Wastes (seq B)
**Look:** a vast valley of grey ash dunes; colossal fallen stone statues of ancient kings (heads and broken hands 10-20 m tall) half-buried; dead black trees; constant soft ashfall like snow; far mountains fading into haze; a dead pale sun disc low in a grey-white sky.
**Light:** overcast, soft cold top light (cool grey, 6500 K) as ambient fill; the lantern is the warm key on Astra's face and chest (from below/side); her blue LEDs and the blue energy blade give cyan rim/slash light. Atmosphere: heavy ash particles, depth haze. Palette: desaturated grey-blue world, one warm amber point, cyan accents.
**Wraiths:** tall faceless humanoid figures made of drifting dark grey ash, glowing ember-orange eyes, edges dissolving into smoke; they burst into ash clouds when cut.

## L3. The Broken Bridge (seq C)
**Look:** a narrow ancient stone bridge with broken balustrades and weathered carved pillars, spanning a bottomless chasm between two cliffs; sections missing; stones crumbling into darkness; ash storm with wind-driven ash.
**Light:** stormy dusk. Backlight = intermittent lightning flashes inside the ash clouds (cold white-violet), giving strong silhouettes and rim light. Key = warm lantern on her face when the flame lives; cyan from LEDs and blade. Dark blue-grey ambient. Atmosphere: wind-blown ash streaks, sparks. Palette: near-black blues, white lightning, amber, cyan.

## L4. The World's Edge / Sun Cradle (seq D)
**Look:** a high rocky cliff top at the edge of the world, above an endless sea of clouds; a colossal ancient stone ring (30 m tall, carved with sun runes) standing upright at the cliff edge, a wide brass brazier on a stone dais at its foot. Ash has stopped; the air is clear and still.
**Light:** first half (D01-D07): blue hour, deep blue sky, last stars, cool soft ambient; Astra's dimming LEDs are the only cyan light on her; low contrast, quiet. Second half (D08-D10): the ring ignites with white-gold light, then a new sun rises behind the ring: hard warm golden key (3200 K) raking across her face from the side/behind, long shadows, god rays through the ring, cloud sea glowing gold and pink. Palette: blue → gold.
