Image → 1-hour ambient loop video

Give Claude one image. It analyses the scene, animates only what should move (snow, fire, steam…), builds a seamless 10-second loop, composes original piano music with quiet ambience, and delivers a one-hour 1080p video plus the audio and the silent loop.

The seamless 10-second picture master (2.8 MB). Re-mux with any audio for a new long video.

The nine stages

Claude decides · Script renders and measures · You sign off. Every stage has a numeric gate.

0Preflight

ScriptClaude

Models, disk, nothing heavy running. Validate draw-things-cli (not yet proven).

1Intake

Claude

Copy the image to input/, make the job folder.

2Scene analysis & masks

Claude

List what moves; crop 16:9; draw animated, lock and QC regions on real edges → job.json.

Gate: overlay reviewed; polygons on real edges

3Prompt

Claude

Strict "cinemagraph" template: a locked photograph where only the named things move. Never mention the camera.

4Drift probe

ScriptClaude

3 seeds × 121 frames. Measure camera drift and whether the effects really move.

Gate: drift ≤ 0.7 px on ≥ 2 seeds · snow ↓ ≥ 65 % · steam ↑ ≥ 60 %

5Two clips A & B

Script

145 frames each at 1024×576 from the two best seeds. Same image, so they can't drift apart.

6Loop build

ScriptSign-off A

Chain A → B → A with 1 s crossfades = 240 frames (10 s). AI only inside masks; the rest is the original image.

Gate: seam ratio ≤ 1.3 · locked pixels identical · brightness std ≤ 1.0 · you can't see the loop point

7Music & ambience

ScriptSign-off B

Three 30-second samples (you pick), then the ambience mixed 14 dB under. Explicit tempo and key.

8Full hour

ScriptSign-off C

26 piano pieces crossfaded, slow leveler, ambience, AAC, stream-copied onto 360 loops.

Gate: exactly 3600.000 s · 86,400 frames · ≈ −22.5 LUFS · no near-silence

9Deliver & clean up

Claude

Keep the three files; delete scratch; write the run log; rebuild these pages; commit.

Six rules that made it work

  1. Only what should move comes from AI. Everything else is the original image, pixel-locked by a mask.
  2. Build the loop, don't generate it. Two clips from the same image, chained A → B → A.
  3. Measure the camera first. Models zoom and drift; check on 3 seeds before rendering anything final.
  4. Describe a photograph, not a scene. "One single locked photograph… the only motion is…". Words like "the camera never zooms" made drift worse.
  5. One heavy model at a time on the 64 GB Mac (video, music, LLMs never together).
  6. Numbers gate every stage; Claude can't hear or watch, so you make the three sign-offs.
